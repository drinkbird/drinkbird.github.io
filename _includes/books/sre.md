Google's Site Reliability Engineering brings software engineering principles to the work of operating production systems. It covers the practices behind reliable services: service-level objectives, error budgets, monitoring, incident response, and learning from failures.

The completely rewritten 2nd edition (2026) explains how Google's SRE practices have evolved over the past decade, including cloud reliability and the impact of AI on production environments. It is a practical companion to [[devops]] and [[releaseit]] for teams responsible for both shipping software and keeping it running.
